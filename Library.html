Inquiry support

Help with the research, not just the shelf

Bring a half-formed question. The library team works alongside teachers on units of inquiry, personal projects and extended essays — from framing a research question to getting the citations right.

IB Career-related Programme

The reflective project

The CP core piece: an ethical dilemma arising from your career-related study, argued over roughly 3,000 words. We support all of it — narrowing the dilemma, finding sources that argue against you, keeping the RPPF entries honest, and holding the three supervision checkpoints.

  • Choosing an ethical dilemma

IB Diploma Programme

The extended essay

Four thousand words of independent research in a subject you choose — the closest thing to university work you will do at school. The library runs the research strand alongside your supervisor: turning a topic into an arguable question, finding scholarly sources beyond the first page of results, and keeping the RRS honest as you go.

About Learners Cove

Learners Cove, the Academy library, sits at the centre of the Academy's nine-acre campus in the Financial District, overlooking the lake. Opened with the school in 2022, it serves readers from Early Years to Grade 10 across an IB continuum.

Our work is simple to state and harder to do: put the right resource in a young reader's hands at the moment they are curious, and teach them how to find the next one themselves.

Borrowing rules, copyright guidance and the acceptable-use policy are in the library handbook.
